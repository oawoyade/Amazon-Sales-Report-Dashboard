# 🛒 Amazon Sales Report Dashboard (2024)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive analysis of Amazon's sales performance for the year 2024. The aim is to uncover actionable insights into order statuses, fulfillment patterns, shipping providers, product popularity, size preferences, regional performance, and customer purchasing behavior. The dashboard provides stakeholders with a clear view of what’s driving revenue and where operational improvements can be made across logistics, product offerings, and customer segmentation.

---

## 🗂️ Data Source

This dataset is a curated extract of transactional e-commerce data, reflecting Amazon’s sales performance over the 2024 calendar year. It includes variables such as order status, product category, courier provider, SKU, product size, city, country, shipping method, and sales quantity. The data was prepared and anonymized for analysis and visualization purposes.

## 📊 Dashboard Structure

The dashboard is organized into multiple interactive sections to provide a holistic view of business operations:

- **Total Orders**: Over **1 million units sold**
- **Top Product**: **USB Cable**
- **Top Size Sold**: **Medium**
- **Top Performing Category**: **Set**

- **Top Sales City**: **Bengaluru**

---

###  Product & Category Breakdown
- Most popular SKUs: `J0230-SKD-M` and `JNE3797-KR-L`
- Highest-selling categories: **Set**, **Kurta**, **Western Dress**

### Fulfillment & Shipping Analysis
- **Amazon Fulfillment** contributed to 69% of total shipments
- **Merchant Fulfillment** handled the remaining 31%
- Most orders are **Expedited Shipping (69%)**, followed by **Standard (31%)**
- **FedEx** processed over **282,000 shipments**, followed closely by **Royal Mail** and **DHL**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 🧍 Product & Customer Behavior
- **Apparel and Set categories** consistently outperform other items
- Sizes **M**, **L**, and **XL** account for over 70% of product sales
- SKU-level insights reveal specific product codes driving volume

### 🌍 Regional and Location Insights
- **Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Mumbai** drive over 30% of revenue
- Postal codes in urban metros outperform smaller locations, indicating market maturity
  
### 🚚 Fulfillment and Shipping Trends
- Customers strongly prefer **Expedited Shipping**
- **Amazon's own delivery network** outperforms third-party providers in volume
- FedEx is the most reliable third-party partner for high-volume deliveries

---

## ✅ Recommendations

1. **Expand inventory of top SKUs** such as `J0230-SKD-M` and `JNE3797-KR-L` to meet increasing demand, especially in popular sizes (M and L).
2. **Strengthen distribution centers** in Bengaluru and Hyderabad to shorten delivery time and reduce shipping costs in these high-demand regions.
3. **Continue promoting Set and Kurta categories** through targeted ads, as they account for the largest category-based sales volumes.
4. **Evaluate pricing and shipping strategy for merchant-fulfilled orders** to bring service levels closer to Amazon-fulfilled orders.
5. **Optimize the Expedited Delivery pipeline**, given its overwhelming preference by customers, to reduce logistics costs while maintaining speed.
